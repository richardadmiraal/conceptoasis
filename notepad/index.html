Each coupon page holds nine coupons. The other pages are lined note pages.

Each coupon carries a QR code and its own short code. When coupon.recomemo opens, merchants scan it to redeem, and the RCM ledger counts each use: once, twice, or again and again. Until then the QR codes point to coupon.recomemo.com. Sample coupons are made up; swap in real merchants who’ve agreed to their offer.
