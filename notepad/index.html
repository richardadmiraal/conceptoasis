The pad

Add as many coupon pages as you like with + Coupon page above the sheet. Each one can carry twelve different coupons.

Each coupon carries a QR code and a short code. When coupon.recomemo opens, the merchant scans it to redeem, and the RCM ledger counts every use: once, twice, or again and again. Until then the QR codes point to coupon.recomemo.com. Sample pills are made up.

Print order co|co arranges it

co|co collects the print cost, splits it between the organizer and the merchants, and sends the PDF to our print partner. What the printer does:

  • Prints the PDF single-sided on letter paper, landscape (11 × 8.5 in), one sheet per page, in page order.
  • Collates each set of 20 sheets and cuts along the two red dashed lines into three strips, about 3.67 × 8.5 in.
  • Pads each strip with glue at the top edge and a cardboard back, making three notepads per set.
  • Ships the finished pads to the organizer for neighbourhood delivery.
