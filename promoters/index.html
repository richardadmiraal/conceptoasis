What groups ask for

  • Reels and short video for the group's page
  • Photography of homes, people and places
  • Social posts and copy that sound like the group

  • Local events and launches
How it works

The group pays through recomemo. You do the work and earn your fee.

1 · Sign up

Tell us what you offer and where, with three links to your work. Your promoter profile is a recomemo, verified U + 3: you, plus three links.

2 · Get matched

Groups of 12 see promoters in their area and choose who they work with. Bring a group to SharedPromoPage yourself and you earn a finder’s fee for it.

3 · Get paid

The group pays through recomemo.com, co|co collects, and pays you your share. No chasing invoices.

Fees are set out in your promoter agreement. Your fee is for promotion services and for bringing groups to SharedPromoPage, never for real estate referrals or leads.
