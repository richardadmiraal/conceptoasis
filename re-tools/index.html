How it works

Five quick, fun steps. No calls unless they ask.

1 · About

Detached, attached or commercial, then the house type. Enter an address and C-KAI finds the area, maps nearby active listings and loads local key words.

2 · Key words

Picture pills for what makes the place special. Search or add their own, and describe any of them in their own words.

3 · Features

House style, living room, dining & entry, outbuildings and suites, each in its own coloured folder. Pick the active listings most like theirs.

4 · Send to

Email, text or WhatsApp, plus their choice of agent. Not intended to solicit existing agency relationships.

5 · Review

Optional walkthrough with an agent, consent, and send. Then Part 1 of the CMA Report is ready as a PDF.

Hard to lose

Saved on their device, "Welcome back" restores, and the back button asks before leaving. Links out stay hidden until the report is sent.

CMA Report · Part 1 of 4

An instant report, with the agent's part still to come

  • Part 1, Live Comp Report: their details, key words, features, the active listings they picked with photos and live links, and a median asking price with a plain-language disclaimer.
  • Parts 2 to 4, from the agent: sold comparables, the price range and strategy, and supporting documents.
  • Save as PDF, share, email, and refresh listings while it's open.
  • "We won't know for sure until we walk through your property." A walkthrough button on every report.

Asking prices are not sale prices. Part 1 is not a valuation or an appraisal. Sold data is provided privately by the agent.
