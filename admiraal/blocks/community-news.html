WHL hockey is back

Meet the Chilliwack Rapids

Major junior hockey is coming home. On Monday, November 23, the WHL's newest team was introduced at the Chilliwack Coliseum as the Chilliwack Rapids, named for the fast, powerful rivers of the Fraser Valley. The stylized "C" logo puts our mountains on top and the rapids below. The Rapids join the league's BC Division as its 24th team for the 2027-28 season, with Jeremy Colliton as general manager and head coach. It's the first WHL team in town since the Bruins left in 2011, and we can't wait to fill the 5,000-seat Coliseum.

Sources: CHL · Chilliwack Progress
Hometown hero

From Chilliwack Minor Hockey to the NHL: Zach Benson

Born in Chilliwack in 2005, Zach Benson learned the game in Chilliwack Minor Hockey, moved on to Yale Hockey Academy, then starred in the WHL with the Winnipeg Ice. The Buffalo Sabres drafted him 13th overall in 2023. When kids lace up at our rinks this winter, that's the path right in front of them, and soon a WHL team to watch at home.

Cheer on the Chiefs and the Jets
