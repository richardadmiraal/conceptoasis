recomemo spotcoupon.recomemo

An ad spot for any website.

A recomemo spot shows the network’s coupon ads as pills, one at a time, and links each to its offer. Merchants change the online offer in coupon.recomemo, and every spot shows the new value.

Live: the admiraal.ca spot

Add it to admiraal.ca (RealtyNinja)

<div class="recomemo-spot" data-place="admiraal.ca"></div>
<script src="https://conceptoasis.com/assets/js/recomemo-spot.js" defer></script>

  1. In RealtyNinja, open the page where the spot should go (or the site-wide footer, if your plan has custom code there).
  2. Add a custom HTML or embed block where you want the ad.
  3. Paste the two lines and save. The spot appears and rotates every 7 seconds.

Other sites use the same two lines with their own data-place, so each site shows only the ads chosen for it.
