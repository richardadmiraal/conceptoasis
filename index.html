The Live Comp Report

A CMA request that's fun to fill in. Owners build their own property report: key words, features, the active listings most like theirs. It comes together live beside the form, and the agent finishes the CMA.

  • Enter an address. The area, nearby active listings and local key words load on their own.
  • Colourful pills for key words and features, with a picture in each and room for their own words.
  • Pick active comps or add MLS® numbers, and the report shows a median asking price, with a clear disclaimer.
  • Part 1 of the CMA Report comes out as a branded PDF with live links. The agent adds sold data, the price range and documents.
  • C-KAI, the AI assistant, offers suggestions. Saved on the device, and hard to lose.
